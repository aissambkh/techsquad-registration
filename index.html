The club

What is TechSquad?

TechSquad is the scientific club of the Higher School of Applied Sciences of Tlemcen (ESSA). We are a team of students passionate about technology who organize workshops, hackathons and projects, and bring student life to the next level with energy and good vibes.

Questions

Still hesitating?

I'm a beginner, is that a problem?

Not at all! TechSquad welcomes every level. We learn together, and the more experienced members help the others.

I'm already a member, what should I do?

Simply choose "Re-registration" at the top of the form and fill in your details.

What happens after I register?

The team will contact you by email very soon with the next steps. Keep a close eye on your inbox!

Can I take part with a busy schedule?

Yes! Everyone gets involved at their own pace. What matters is being motivated and enjoying the adventure.

Where can I meet the team in person?

Come to our Welcome Day on 13/10/2026 at ESSAT, in the Main Hall near the library entrance.
